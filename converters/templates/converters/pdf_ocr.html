{% trans "What does OCR actually stand for?" %}

{% trans "OCR stands for Optical Character Recognition. It is a technology that recognizes text within a digital image. It is commonly used to recognize text in scanned documents and photos, converting a physical paper record into editable digital data." %}

{% trans "Can the tool read handwritten notes?" %}
